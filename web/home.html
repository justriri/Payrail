How Payrail works

Four steps from a wallet to a verified response.

1

Discover and choose an API

Browse listings with a clear price, a written response contract and what counts as a qualifying result.

2

Fund in a supported currency

Connect and verify your wallet, then add funds. Supported assets are converted to USDT through verified routes, with the rate and fee shown before you approve.

3

Pay only for qualifying responses

Each request is checked against the contract. Qualifying responses are charged; failures and stale results are not.

4

Publish and monetize

Create a listing, prove you own the endpoint, set a price and a contract, test it, and publish when every requirement is met.

5

Track everything

Usage, spending, receipts, earnings, platform fees and payouts are computed from saved records and the ledger.

6

Get paid in your currency

Choose USDT or another supported payout asset, prove you control the destination address, review the exact quote, and confirm.

Built for both sides of the API

Clear terms for the people who pay and the people who get paid.

For buyers and agents

  • Charged only when the response meets the contract
  • Fund with the assets you hold, converted to USDT on verified routes
  • Receipts for every request and a statement that reconciles
  • Spending limits and revocable keys for AI agents

For sellers

  • Prove endpoint ownership before anything is public
  • Test against your own contract, then publish
  • Earnings and a 3% platform fee shown before you sell
  • Payouts in USDT or a supported asset, to an address you verify
